Privacy Policy for 口袋里的鬼谷子

Effective date: 2026-11-04

Operator / data controller: Qayvi LLC

1. Scope and operator

口袋里的鬼谷子 ("Guiguzi" below) is operated by Qayvi LLC. It helps you review text from conversations you choose to provide, using artificial intelligence. This policy explains local processing, online analysis, and your choices. Contact email: {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

2. Information processed on your device

Screenshots are recognized on your device with ML Kit OCR. Imported audio is transcribed on your device. The analysis request contains text, not the original image or audio file. You can review and mask the text first; masking does not guarantee that all identifying information has been removed. Temporary copies can remain when cleanup fails. Originals in your photo library or other apps are not deleted by our cleanup.

Saving an analysis is manual. Saved records contain the confirmed text, result, record identifier, date, format version, and interface language, encrypted on this device. Backup and device-migration exclusions are configured; actual results can differ by operating system and how the app was installed. Clearing the current conversation does not delete saved records. Removing the app normally removes its local files. Copied replies remain in the system clipboard outside our deletion controls.

3. Online text and account information

When you accept the processing notice and submit an analysis, the confirmed text and selected routing information are sent to our configured backend with a session credential, consent version, and request identifier. The server creates a random account identifier. We do not collect an email address, telephone number, or a Google sign-in identity. The analysis context contains the selected interface/reply languages and anonymous speaker roles; it does not send the account identifier, session credential, or device identifier to the model.

The backend uses the text to generate a result and keeps usage counts and a hash of each completed request to prevent repeated charging. A hash linked to an account is not anonymous data. Text is not automatically saved as an analysis history on the backend, but an unfinished operation can retain it after a timeout. Section 8 describes the model provider. This policy does not promise zero retention by the model provider or that the text is not used for training.

4. Device summary

The app derives a device summary with a one-way hash of ANDROID_ID combined with an app namespace. The raw ANDROID_ID is not sent to our backend. The hashed summary goes only to our backend and is not sent to the analysis model. We use the summary to prevent repeated free allowances and to limit additional analysis attempts after an allowance is exhausted. It is a device identifier, not proof of identity or anonymous data.

The summary may remain stable across reinstallations, but operating-system changes, resets, or other conditions can change it. Deleting your account or withdrawing analysis consent does not erase the independent device allowance and attempt records. A scheduled sweep deletes device free-use records 730 days after the device last used the service, and safety-attempt records 35 days after the UTC day they cover. Those records contain counts only, no conversation text, and can remain after the random account is deleted until the sweep removes them.

5. Purchases in this release

This release does not include in-app purchases, subscriptions, or subscription restore, and it does not integrate RevenueCat. The app does not send an account identifier, product, store receipt, or purchase token to any payment provider. There is no card-number collection form. Deleting the account does not refund any Google Play transaction, and this release creates no store subscription.

6. Retention periods

7. Deletion and withdrawal

You can clear the current conversation, delete individual saved records, remove speech models in their settings, or request account deletion in the app. These actions have different scopes. Account deletion attempts local cleanup and backend deletion; offline or failed operations may be queued and retried when the app starts or returns to the foreground. A server saying it no longer has an account mapping is not evidence that third-party data has been deleted. Some local credential cleanup failures also require retry. A public account-deletion page is available at the address listed in the store entry; a matched server-side deletion request completes within 30 days, and if the account cannot be found we reply that it was not found.

You can decline further analysis. Declining only changes local state; the app does not yet send that withdrawal to the server. Withdrawal does not guarantee cancellation of a request already in progress, and it does not automatically delete saved records or quota records. Any lawful retention exceptions are limited to the records described in section 6; device records are not silently covered by a claim to delete everything.

8. Service providers and international processing

Hugging Face and its delivery network currently host downloadable speech models; downloading exposes connection information such as IP addresses, but our download request does not contain your conversations or audio. The production speech-model download host may change; if it does, this policy will be updated. Local ML Kit and on-device transcription processing is separate from online conversation analysis.

The data path is phone → InstaBox server in the United States → Alibaba Cloud Bailian in Beijing, China, using HTTPS on remote connections. Session credentials, consent records, and device summaries reach the US server; only confirmed conversation text and analysis context continue to Beijing, without session credentials or device summaries. This involves cross-border processing in the US and China.

The online analysis provider is Alibaba Cloud Bailian (阿里云百炼), using its Beijing processing region. For users outside China, the confirmed conversation text and analysis context are transferred across borders to China for processing. Qwen generates the answer and GLM performs the semantic precheck. How long Alibaba Cloud Bailian retains this text, and whether it uses it for training, follow our agreement with Alibaba Cloud and its own policies; we have not yet verified that period, and this policy does not promise zero retention or no training by the provider.

9. Security, logs, and tracking

Saved analyses and deletion credentials use encryption on the device. Backend request logs are limited to route, status, response size, duration, and a fixed error code where needed; the reviewed logger omits conversation text, account/session credentials, and IP addresses. Native components still produce some local operational logs, including language and filesystem information. Hosting/network logs and SDK behavior need separate verification. We cannot claim that there are no logs.

No advertising, data-broker, or cross-app advertising-tracking integration was found in the reviewed application code. This is not a claim that providers receive no technical information.

10. Your rights and processing grounds

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, or object to processing and complain to a supervisory authority. Use the contact channel above. Requests may require identity verification, and lawful retention exceptions are limited to those described in this policy.

The processing notice governs your choice to submit text for analysis. Device allowance control relies on legitimate interests where permitted, limited to the counts described above.

11. Adults only

The service is intended for people aged 18 or older. Do not submit material you are not entitled to provide, and remove unnecessary information about other people, especially children. The app does not verify age; this policy is not evidence of an implemented age gate. Analysis results are generated by artificial intelligence, may be wrong or incomplete, and are not professional advice.

Accounts without sign-in

The server assigns an anonymous installation ID without collecting email or third-party sign-in identities. This is a linkable pseudonymous identifier, not fully anonymous data. Sessions are memory-only and must be reissued after restart. Deletion credentials are encrypted locally; the server's SQLite ledger stores only credential hashes and IDs needed for deletion. Account deletion clears local content and asks the server to delete the server identity; offline requests are retried when the app next opens online. Device free-use records remain under the retention rules in section 6 and are not reset by reissuance or deletion.
