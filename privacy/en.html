1. Scope and operator

口袋里的鬼谷子 (Android package com.guiguzi.app, called Guiguzi below) is operated by Qayvi LLC. Guiguzi helps you review text from conversations you choose to provide. This policy explains local processing, online analysis, subscriptions, and your choices. Contact email: [contact email].

2. Information processed on your device

Screenshots and imported audio are processed on your device for text recognition or transcription. The analysis request contains text, not the original image or audio file. You can review and mask the text first; masking does not guarantee that all identifying information has been removed. Temporary copies can remain when cleanup fails. Originals in your photo library or other apps are not deleted by our cleanup.

Saving an analysis is manual. Saved records contain the confirmed text, result, record identifier, date, format version, and interface language, encrypted on this device. Backup and device-migration exclusions are configured; actual results can differ by operating system and how the app was installed. Clearing the current conversation does not delete saved records. Deleting your account clears saved analyses on this device and destroys the keys that unlock them. Downloaded speech models are not normally removed by account deletion. Removing the app normally removes its local files; iOS Offload App retains documents and data. Copied replies remain in the system clipboard outside our deletion controls.

3. Online text and account information

When you accept the processing notice and submit an analysis, the confirmed text and selected routing information are sent to our configured backend with a session credential, consent version, and request identifier. The server creates a random account identifier. We do not collect an email address, telephone number, or Apple or Google sign-in identity. The analysis context contains the selected interface and reply languages and anonymous speaker roles. The account identifier, session credential, and device identifier are not sent to the model.

The backend uses the text to generate a result and keeps usage counts and a hash of each completed request to prevent repeated charging. A hash linked to an account is not anonymous data. Text is not automatically saved as an analysis history on the backend, but an unfinished operation can retain it after a timeout. Section 8 describes how the model provider retains data and whether it may use data for training. This policy does not promise zero retention or that the text is not used for training.

4. Device summary (only if the device-quota feature is included)

If the device-quota feature is included, the app derives a device summary using a one-way hash of an app-specific value: a random identifier held in the iOS Keychain, or the Android system identifier combined with an app namespace. Raw Android identifiers are not sent to our backend. The summary goes only to our backend, not to RevenueCat or the analysis model. We use it to prevent repeated free allowances and to limit additional analysis attempts after an allowance is exhausted. It is a device identifier, not proof of identity or anonymous data.

The summary may remain stable across reinstallations, but operating-system changes, resets, or other conditions can change it. Deleting an account or withdrawing analysis consent does not erase the independent device allowance and attempt records. Those records are kept for [device-quota retention period].

5. Purchases and RevenueCat

If you enter a purchase or restore flow, RevenueCat processes our account identifier, products, store receipts or purchase tokens, subscription status, and operational device and application information. It supports purchase validation, access to paid features, fraud prevention, and purchase analytics. Automatic advertising and device-identifier collection and SDK diagnostics are disabled in our integration; this does not eliminate ordinary network metadata. Chat text, images, audio, and account-deletion credentials are not passed to RevenueCat by our code.

RevenueCat customer records can include the transaction country, or a country inferred from an IP address, and recent connection times. Apple or Google handles payment details in its store; our app has no card-number collection form. Deleting your app account does not cancel a store subscription or erase the store's transaction records. Manage renewal in the store's subscription settings.

7. Deletion and withdrawal

You can clear the current conversation, delete individual saved records, remove speech models in their settings, or request account deletion in the app. These actions have different scopes. Account deletion attempts local cleanup and backend deletion. Offline or failed operations may be queued and retried when the app starts or returns to the foreground. A server response that it no longer has an account mapping is not evidence that third-party data has been deleted. Some local credential cleanup failures also require a retry.

You can decline further analysis. Declining updates the choice on this device only. It does not notify our servers, it does not guarantee cancellation of a request already in progress, and it does not delete saved records, quota records, or store transactions. You can also request deletion as described on the Account & Data Deletion page, or email [contact email]. For a request that can be matched to an account, the processing time is [deletion processing time]. Data kept under a legal exception is limited to what this policy describes. Account deletion does not erase device-quota records, and it is not a deletion of every record.

8. Service providers and international processing

RevenueCat supports subscriptions. Apple and Google operate their stores. Hugging Face and its delivery network currently host downloadable speech models. Downloading exposes connection information such as IP addresses, but our download request does not contain your conversations or audio. On-device Vision, ML Kit, and Whisper processing is separate from online conversation analysis.

Online analysis follows this path: phone → InstaBox server in the United States → Alibaba Cloud Bailian in Beijing, China. Remote connections use HTTPS. Session credentials, consent records, and device summaries reach the US server. Only confirmed conversation text and analysis context continue to Beijing. Session credentials and device summaries are not sent to the model. This path involves processing in the United States and in China. InstaBox may keep connection logs for [logs and third-party retention period].

The online analysis provider is Alibaba Cloud Bailian, in its Beijing region. For users outside China, confirmed conversation text and analysis context are transferred to China for processing. Qwen generates the answer and GLM performs the semantic precheck. The model provider's retention period is [model-provider retention period]. This policy does not promise zero retention or that this content is not used for training.

9. Security, logs, and tracking

Saved analyses and deletion credentials are encrypted on the device. Backend request logs are limited to route, status, response size, duration, and a fixed error code where needed. Server logs do not include conversation text, account or session credentials, or IP addresses. Native components can still write local operational logs, including language and file paths. Hosting logs, network logs, and SDK behavior are not necessarily the same as those server logs. We do not claim that there are no logs, and we do not claim that every connection is encrypted.

The app does not include advertising, data-broker, or cross-app advertising tracking. Service providers can still receive technical information.

11. Adults only

The service is for people aged 18 or older. Do not submit material you are not entitled to provide, and remove unnecessary information about other people, especially children. The service does not currently verify age. Stating that it is for people 18 or older is not an age gate.

Accounts without sign-in

The server assigns an installation identifier and does not collect an email address or a third-party sign-in identity. The identifier can be linked to a session. It is a pseudonymous identifier, not fully anonymous data. Sessions are stored only in memory and must be issued again after the server restarts. Deletion credentials are encrypted on the device. The server stores a hash of the credential and the identifiers needed to finish deletion. Account deletion clears local content and revokes the identity on the server. If the device is offline, the request is sent the next time you open the app online. Device free-use records are kept for [device-quota retention period] and are not reset when a new installation identifier is issued or when the account is deleted.
