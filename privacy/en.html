Guiguzi Privacy Policy — draft

Internal review draft, 2026-10-25. Not ready for publication. Device-related provisions apply only if the reviewed device-quota feature is included in the released app. Resolve all pending items against the actual release before publishing.

1. Scope and operator

Guiguzi helps you review text from conversations you choose to provide. This policy explains local processing, online analysis, subscriptions, and your choices. The operator and working contact address must be supplied above before release.

The beta deployment target is an InstaBox server in the United States, with real v9 analysis using Alibaba Cloud Bailian in Beijing. Purchases and subscription restoration are disabled during beta. Release sign-in is not yet integrated: a buildable package is not a working end-to-end service. Simulated development mode remains local-development-only. This work does not establish an actual deployment, production accounts, or validated payments.

2. Information processed on your device

Screenshots and imported audio are processed on your device for text recognition or transcription. The analysis request contains text, not the original image or audio file. You can review and mask the text first; masking does not guarantee that all identifying information has been removed. Temporary copies can remain when cleanup fails. Originals in your photo library or other apps are not deleted by our cleanup.

Saving an analysis is manual. Saved records contain the confirmed text, result, record identifier, date, format version, and interface language, encrypted on this device. Backup and migration exclusions are configured but still require release-device verification. Clearing the current conversation does not delete saved records. Removing the app normally removes its local files; iOS Offload App retains documents and data. Copied replies remain in the system clipboard outside our deletion controls.

3. Online text and account information

When you accept the processing notice and submit an analysis, the confirmed text and selected routing information are sent to our configured backend with a session credential, consent version, and request identifier. The server creates a random account identifier. The reviewed code does not collect an email address, telephone number, or Apple/Google sign-in identity through a working login service. The analysis context contains the selected interface/reply languages and anonymous speaker roles; it does not send the account identifier, session credential, or device identifier to the model.

The backend uses the text to generate a result and keeps usage counts and a hash of each completed request to prevent repeated charging. A hash linked to an account is not anonymous data. Text is not automatically saved as an analysis history on the backend, but an unfinished operation can retain it after a timeout. Production model processing, retention, and any training use remain to be confirmed before online release.

4. Device identifier — conditional release feature

If the device-quota feature is included, the app derives a device summary using a one-way hash of an app-specific value: a random identifier held in the iOS Keychain, or the Android system identifier combined with an app namespace. Raw Android identifiers are not sent to our backend. The summary goes only to our backend, not to RevenueCat or the analysis model. We use it to prevent repeated free allowances and to limit additional analysis attempts after an allowance is exhausted. It is a device identifier, not proof of identity or anonymous data.

The summary may remain stable across reinstallations, but operating-system changes, resets, or other conditions can change it. Deleting an account or withdrawing analysis consent does not erase the independent device allowance and attempt records. The reviewed implementation has no automatic expiry for those records. Pending before release: a specific retention period, deletion procedure, and justification for the exception.

5. Purchases and RevenueCat

When real subscriptions are enabled and you enter a purchase or restore flow, RevenueCat processes our account identifier, products, store receipts or purchase tokens, subscription status, and operational device/application information. It supports purchase validation, access to paid features, fraud prevention, and purchase analytics. Automatic advertising/device-identifier collection and SDK diagnostics are disabled in our integration; this does not eliminate ordinary network metadata. Chat text, images, audio, and account-deletion credentials are not passed to RevenueCat by our code.

RevenueCat's customer documentation describes transaction country or country inferred from an IP address, and recent connection times. The actual project settings and this country processing need confirmation before submission. Apple or Google handles payment details in its store; our app has no card-number collection form. Deleting your app account does not cancel a store subscription or erase the store's transaction records; manage renewal in the store's subscription settings.

6. Retention periods

Information Reviewed retention and release conditions
Unsaved text, screenshots, and audio copies Kept for the current workflow; cleanup is attempted on relevant clear/delete/startup paths. Failed cleanup or unfinished analysis can leave temporary data; there is no guaranteed immediate erasure time.
Saved analyses and downloaded speech models Saved analyses remain until deleted. Valid models remain until removed in model settings or with app files; account deletion does not normally remove them.
Sessions, usage records, and request hashes Session access expires after 24 hours by default, but records have no automatic expiry; account deletion or a server restart clears the reviewed in-memory records. Production retention must be specified.
Local deletion credentials and pending requests Encrypted and retained until confirmation and successful local cleanup; no fixed automatic expiry. Retrying requires the app to run again with connectivity.
Minimal consent proof after deletion A session-token hash, notice version, decision, decision time, and app version; expires 365 days after the latest consent/refusal decision, not 365 days after deletion. The prototype checks expiry at startup and normally hourly; a restart can remove it earlier.
Deletion confirmation record A confirmed deletion receipt is retained indefinitely: only the deletion-credential hash, first confirmation time, and the fact that deletion was confirmed. It contains no account ID, session-token hash, device link, or content. This lets a device holding a deletion credential with no expiry confirm deletion years later. The separate in-memory local-deletion marker and development-only receipt for an unconfigured provider still expire after 365 days; neither proves provider deletion.
Device and daily quota records, if enabled Device free-use and safety-attempt records survive account deletion and have no implemented expiry. Paid daily records are removed on successful account deletion, but old daily rows otherwise have no expiry. The period and reliable deletion across restarts remain pending.
Logs and third-party records App/host/operating-system log retention and the applicable RevenueCat, store, download-host, and future model retention periods require confirmation. No universal 365-day or immediate-deletion promise applies to them.

7. Deletion and withdrawal

You can clear the current conversation, delete individual saved records, remove speech models in their settings, or request account deletion in the app. These actions have different scopes. Account deletion attempts local cleanup and backend deletion; offline or failed operations may be queued and retried when the app starts or returns to the foreground. A server saying it no longer has an account mapping is not evidence that third-party data has been deleted. Some local credential cleanup failures also require retry.

You can decline further analysis. In the reviewed app, declining only changes local state; the backend can record a withdrawal, but the app does not yet send that notification. Withdrawal does not guarantee cancellation of a request already in progress, and it does not automatically delete saved records, quota records, or store transactions. Pending before publication: a working public account-deletion webpage, a contact channel, and verifiable completion times. Any lawful retention exceptions must be explained and limited; device records are not silently covered by a claim to delete everything.

8. Service providers and international processing

RevenueCat supports subscriptions. Apple and Google operate their stores. Hugging Face and its delivery network currently host downloadable speech models; downloading exposes connection information such as IP addresses, but our download request does not contain your conversations or audio. The production speech-model download host remains to be finalized. Local Vision/ML Kit/Whisper processing is separate from online conversation analysis.

The beta data path is phone → InstaBox server in the United States → Alibaba Cloud Bailian in Beijing, China, using HTTPS on remote connections. Session credentials, consent records, and device summaries reach the US server; only confirmed conversation text and analysis context continue to Beijing, without session credentials or device summaries. This involves cross-border processing in the US and China. InstaBox hosting, connection logs, and retention policies require separate verification.

Internal testing only; review again before public store release. The online analysis provider is Alibaba Cloud Bailian (阿里云百炼), using its Beijing processing region. For users outside China, the confirmed conversation text and analysis context are transferred across borders to China for processing. Qwen generates the answer and GLM performs the semantic precheck. Applicable contracts, subprocessors, transfer safeguards, retention, deletion, and training restrictions remain subject to verification before release; this draft makes no zero-retention or no-training promise. RevenueCat processing terms and locations also require separate verification.

9. Security, logs, and tracking

Saved analyses and deletion credentials use encryption on the device. Backend request logs are limited to route, status, response size, duration, and a fixed error code where needed; the reviewed logger omits conversation text, account/session credentials, and IP addresses. Native components still produce some local operational logs, including language and filesystem information. Hosting/network logs and SDK behavior need separate verification. We cannot claim that there are no logs or that every current development connection is encrypted.

No advertising, data-broker, or cross-app advertising-tracking integration was found in the reviewed application code. This is not a claim that providers receive no technical information. Pending before release: HTTPS across all remote data paths, production access controls, database/backup protection, and tests of retention and deletion.

10. Your rights and processing grounds

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, or object to processing and complain to a supervisory authority. Use the contact channel above once it has been provided. Requests must account for identity verification and lawful, specifically explained retention exceptions; no working contact channel is implied by this draft.

The processing notice governs your choice to submit text. Subscription handling provides the service you request. Fraud prevention and device allowance control may rely on legitimate interests where permitted, but only after an appropriate necessity and balancing assessment; this draft does not establish that assessment or an exemption from device-access rules. Applicable legal grounds and sensitive information safeguards must be confirmed for each launch market.

11. Adults only

The service is intended for people aged 18 or older. Do not submit material you are not entitled to provide, and remove unnecessary information about other people, especially children. The reviewed code does not verify age; this policy is not evidence of an implemented age gate.

Sign-in-free beta

The beta server assigns an anonymous installation ID without collecting email or third-party sign-in identities. This is a linkable pseudonymous identifier, not fully anonymous data. Sessions are memory-only and must be reissued after restart. Deletion credentials are encrypted locally; the existing server SQLite ledger stores only credential hashes and IDs needed for deletion. Account deletion clears local content and revokes the server identity; offline requests are retried when the app next opens online. Device free-use records remain under the existing retention rules and are not reset by reissuance or deletion. If the payment provider is not configured, the app reports that explicitly rather than claiming provider-confirmed deletion.

Material changes to processing must be reflected in the notice and policy before the changed processing begins.
