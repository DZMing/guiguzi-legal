Account & Data Deletion

This page explains how Guiguzi handles account and data deletion: how to delete your account in the app, what is deleted, and what is retained and for how long. It matches the 口袋里的鬼谷子 privacy policy finalized on 2026-11-04.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Guiguzi and tap Menu at the top right of the home screen.
  2. Choose Saved (the app's own copy also calls this the settings entry).
  3. Tap Delete account at the bottom of the page.
  4. Tap Continue.
  5. Tap Delete permanently to confirm a second time.

Deletion immediately removes the sign-in information and all saved content on this phone and destroys the keys that unlock them. The account record on the server is deleted afterwards. With no network, the request is queued and sent automatically the next time you open the app while online. Deletion cannot be undone.

Data deleted with your account

  • The sign-in information and session credentials on this phone;
  • All saved analyses on this device and the keys that unlock them;
  • Local chat state and import cache (cleanup can fail and leave temporary copies; the app retries on the next start);
  • The account record on the server, plus that account's current session, usage records, and request hashes (currently in-memory records, cleared on account deletion or a server restart);
  • After the server confirms deletion, the local deletion credentials are removed.

Data retained after account deletion, and retention periods

DataRetention and notes
Saved analyses (on this device)Saved analyses remain until you delete them; account deletion also clears them.
Downloaded speech modelsRemain until you remove them in model settings or delete the app files; account deletion does not normally remove them.
Unsaved text, screenshots, and audio copiesKept for the current workflow; failed cleanup or unfinished analysis can leave temporary data, with no guaranteed immediate erasure time.
Sessions, usage records, and request hashes (server)The session credential stops working after 24 hours by default. The underlying records are deleted after 30 days of inactivity, and immediately when account deletion is confirmed.
Local deletion credentials and pending requestsEncrypted and retained until confirmation and successful local cleanup; no fixed automatic expiry. Retrying requires the app to run again with connectivity.
Minimal consent proof after deletionSession-token hash, notice version, decision, decision time, and app version; expires 365 days after the latest consent/refusal decision, not after account deletion.
Deletion confirmation recordRetained indefinitely with no expiry; it contains only the deletion-credential hash, the first confirmation time, and the fact that deletion was confirmed — no account ID, session-token hash, device link, or content.
Device and daily quota recordsDevice free-use records are deleted 730 days after the device last used the service; safety-attempt records 35 days after the UTC day they cover. They survive account deletion until the scheduled sweep. This release creates no paid daily records.
Logs and third-party recordsOur own request logs are deleted after 30 days by default. App, operating-system, hosting, Google Play, download-host, and model-provider logs follow each provider's own retention policy.
Store transactionsThis release has no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Deleting your app account does not refund any Google Play transaction.

Request deletion without using the app

If you cannot use the in-app flow, you can request deletion of your account and related data through the contact email: {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. A request that can be matched to an account is completed within 30 days; if the account cannot be found, we reply that it was not found. Please include the random account identifier shown in the app, or we cannot match your email to an account.

An email request covers only account data on our servers. Saved analyses that exist only on your phone cannot be deleted by email; delete them in the app, or delete the app itself.

Other notes

  • Withdrawing analysis consent is not the same as deleting your account, and it does not automatically delete saved content or quota records.
  • The server not finding an account mapping is not proof that third-party data has been deleted. Some local credential cleanup failures also require a retry.
  • If there is no network at deletion time, the app resends the request when it next starts or returns to the foreground; it does not complete while the app is closed.
